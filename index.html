            Answers to your questions

            Moving FAQ

            Our moving company offers a comprehensive range of services, including packing, loading, transportation, unpacking, and assembly for moves, as well as deep cleaning, post-move cleaning, and specialized cleaning for different areas of the home. Pricing is determined based on factors such as property size, move distance, and additional services required, with transparent quotes provided. Our services are fully insured to protect belongings and property. We provide packing materials and recommend scheduling appointments as early as possible to secure preferred dates, with flexibility for last-minute bookings.

            How do you determine pricing?

            Our pricing is determined based on several factors, including the size of your property, the distance of the move, the volume of belongings, and any additional services you may require. We offer transparent pricing and can provide customized quotes to suit your specific needs and budget.

            Are your services insured?

            Yes, we understand the importance of protecting your belongings and property. Our services are fully insured, providing coverage for both moving and cleaning activities. This includes liability coverage for any potential damages or accidents that may occur during the process.

            Do you provide packing materials, or should I purchase them separately?

            We offer a range of packing materials, including boxes, tape, bubble wrap, and protective wrapping, to ensure your belongings are securely packed for the move. These materials can be provided as part of our service package or purchased separately if preferred.

            How far in advance should I schedule my move or cleaning appointment?

            We recommend scheduling your move or cleaning appointment as soon as you have a confirmed date in mind. This helps ensure availability, especially during peak seasons. Typically, we advise booking at least a few weeks in advance to secure your preferred date. However, we can accommodate last-minute bookings whenever possible. Please note our cancellation policy and any associated fees for rescheduling appointments.
